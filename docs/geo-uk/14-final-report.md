Scope agreed with the operator: **audit + infrastructure; nothing is published or deployed.** Routes extend `/uk` (not `/wielka-brytania/`).

## Evidence
- `npm run verify` exit 0 (lint, typecheck, build, all existing guards, new geo guard, fingerprints).
- Production output unchanged against a build of `main`: `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt` byte-identical; the `<body>` HTML of all 41 prerendered pages identical once script/chunk hashes are excluded.
- Guard mutation test: a published record with an unverified census claim, missing gates and forbidden wording ("gwarantowane … bez bólu") fails `test:geo` with each problem listed.
- Preview: draft renders 200 with `noindex, follow` under `GEO_PREVIEW=1`; 404 without the flag.

## What was deliberately not done
No page, no Polish draft copy, no hreflang, no sitemap/llms/link change, no deploy, no new CTA text, no GA4/consent, no keyword volumes or lead forecasts, no census figure marked verified, no clinic claim (two branches, lab, 220–250 patients/month, Polish coordinator) used.

## Findings worth your attention
1. The brief's top-30 TS024 list contains no Boston, Spalding or Wisbech figure, yet Boston/Lincolnshire is a pilot target. It may need to fold into an England hub.
2. Aberdeen's strongest claim is an *ethnic identity* share, a different metric from the *main language* counts used for the other targets; they must not be compared or merged.
3. Region and authority assignments for England, Wales and Northern Ireland are analyst-assigned and need an ONS lookup check.
4. `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` appeared untracked in the working tree after running `next dev`; they were not created by this work and are not committed.
